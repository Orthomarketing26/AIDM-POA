The One To Watch

ClearChoice

Every other name above is a single location local practice. ClearChoice is the exception, a national brand with multiple locations and a comprehensive implant center model (DR 50) running the exact "expert led, all under one roof" positioning AIDM is building around. For implant and full arch searches specifically, this is the one real positioning competitor in the set, not just a local one.

Across all four core commercial terms checked (dentist, dental implants, cosmetic dentist, and orthodontist, all plus Austin), Ahrefs' live SERP snapshot captured zero paid ads. That's a real, repeated signal, not a guarantee (ad presence is far more volatile than organic rank and can shift by time of day or budget pacing), but it matches the zero paid keyword finding on AIDM's own domain. The competitive battle in this market right now is happening in the Google Maps Pack and organic listings, which means Google Business Profile strength and Local Services Ads eligibility matter here just as much as the ad campaigns themselves.

The Procedure

How the budget gets put to work

Google Ads

  • Branded campaign: protect AIDM & residency program searches.
  • Service line ad groups: Cosmetic Dentistry, Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery, Implants & Restorations, General & Emergency, Orthodontics, Pediatric, ranked by real search volume and intent, not evenly split.
  • Maximize Clicks at launch, shifting to Maximize Conversions once each ad group clears 15 to 30 conversions, then tCPA.
  • Landing pages by specialty, not one generic page for every service.

Meta Ads

  • Lead ad campaigns, broad targeting, creative doing the segmentation, not manual audience stacking.
  • Comfort first creative angle: judgment free, anxiety conscious, patient story and facility tour content, matching AIDM's own site positioning directly.
  • Transparent pricing messaging: 0% financing and upfront cost estimates called out explicitly, addressing the #1 objection before it's asked.
  • Retargeting on site visitors and video viewers with the "everything under one roof" convenience message.

Local Services Ads

  • Not funded on day one: see the readiness note below before any LSA budget gets committed.
  • Verification first: Google Screened badge requires business license, liability insurance, and background checks for every treating dentist.
  • Pay per lead, not pay per click: a weekly budget cap, run as its own line once eligible, not folded into the Google Ads tier.
  • General & Emergency first: highest intent categories for calls, fastest way to build the review volume LSA ranking depends on.

Local Services Ads: Readiness

Not day one, but not ignored either

LSA ranks on proximity, review volume, rating, and verification status, not bid amount. AIDM opens with zero Google reviews and an unverified profile. Spending LSA budget on day one means spending it against a profile Google has no trust signal for yet, money that would outperform later for free.

Weeks 1 to 4

Foundation, No Spend

Submit GBP verification, business license, liability insurance, and background checks for every treating dentist. Zero LSA budget committed.

Once Reviews Start Landing

Soft Launch

Turn on LSA for General Dentistry and Emergency only, the categories with the highest intent to call and the fastest trust to build.

Month 3+

Expand

Add Cosmetic, Orthodontics, and Implants to LSA once review volume actually supports ranking in those categories.
