The Procedure

How the budget gets put to work

Google Ads

  • Branded campaign: protect AIDM & residency program searches.
  • Orthodontics ad groups: Traditional Braces, Clear Aligners & Invisalign, Adult Orthodontics, Teen & Phase 1 Orthodontics, split by the same persona logic we use across the portfolio, not one generic ad group.
  • Maximize Clicks at launch, shifting to Maximize Conversions once the account clears 15 to 30 conversions, then tCPA.
  • One landing page path built for orthodontics, not a generic multi service page.

Meta Ads

  • Lead ad campaigns, broad targeting, creative doing the segmentation, not manual audience stacking.
  • Two creative angles: the "Aesthetic Adult" angle (Invisalign, speed of treatment, social confidence) and the "Phase 1 Parent" angle (early intervention, low monthly payments, a child's comfort).
  • Transparent pricing messaging: 0% financing and upfront cost estimates called out explicitly, addressing the #1 objection before it's asked.
  • Retargeting on site visitors and video viewers with the residency trained, institute level expertise angle.

Local Services Ads

  • Not funded on day one: see the readiness note below before any LSA budget gets committed.
  • Verification first: Google Screened badge requires business license, liability insurance, and background checks for every treating dentist.
  • Pay per lead, not pay per click: a weekly budget cap, run as its own line once eligible, not folded into the Google Ads tier.
  • Orthodontic consultations first: the highest intent leads, and the fastest way to build the review volume LSA ranking depends on.

Local Services Ads: Readiness

Not day one, but not ignored either

LSA ranks on proximity, review volume, rating, and verification status, not bid amount. AIDM opens with zero Google reviews and an unverified profile. Spending LSA budget on day one means spending it against a profile Google has no trust signal for yet, money that would outperform later for free.

Weeks 1 to 4

Foundation, No Spend

Submit GBP verification, business license, liability insurance, and background checks for every treating dentist. Zero LSA budget committed.

Once Reviews Start Landing

Soft Launch

Turn on LSA for orthodontic consultation leads specifically, the highest intent to call and the fastest way to build trust with real reviews behind it.

Month 3+

Expand

Widen the LSA service radius and lean the budget toward whichever orthodontic ad group, braces, Invisalign, or adult treatment, is converting best.
